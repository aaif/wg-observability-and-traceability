## Summary

<!-- 2–3 sentences capturing the month at a glance. -->

- Kick started the work on the Observability & Traceability WG charter and deliverables for the next 6 months.
- Held the kick-off meetings for various focus groups that we had planned. The focus groups are now working on their respective deliverables and will be reporting back to the WG as we progress along. We have also identified different individuals to lead the focus groups and help coordinate the efforts. The focus groups are:
  - LLM Primitives Observability
  - Protocol Observability
  - Multi-Agentic Observability (Orchestration & Coordination)
  - Agentic State and Context
  - Agentic Reasoning
- We also also started work on the Use Case and Gap Analysis documents for the WG, which will be shared with the TC and other WGs for feedback and alignment.
