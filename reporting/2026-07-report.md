# [Report] Observability & Traceability – July 2026

## Summary

July focused on execution-model changes and proposal shaping rather than new deliverable publication. To address low participation and overlapping scope, we reduced the focus-group structure from 5 groups to 3 active tracks by merging LLM Primitives with Protocol Observability and Agentic Reasoning with State & Context. 


## Progress Against Objectives

<!-- What shipped, what advanced. Tie back to your stated objectives where possible. -->

- Continued taxonomy coordination with the cross-WG Taxonomy & Landscape work stream, with chairs reviewing universal terms and pushing observability-specific additions.
- Simplified the execution model by consolidating 5 focus groups into 3 active tracks to improve participation and reduce overlap.
- Began shaping the next major technical work items: a cross-boundary observability gap model and the agent behavior trace model recommendation.

### Focus group progress

- LLM Primitives & Protocol Observability (Zayne Turner): 
  - 2 proposals are now under review in the main WG, with a third in progress. 
  - The first one is intending to explore how we can observe the Agentic Resource Discovery (ARD) where we can trace skill discovery, provenance, governance, and pre‑execution trust decisions.
  - The second one is around making Skills as first class citizens in the observability model, and how we can trace skill execution and skill state.
  - The group is also focused simultaneously on progressing the cross-boundary observability gap model.
- Multi-Agentic Observability - Orchestration & Coordination (Ty Smith): 1 proposal is now under review. 
- Agentic State & Context (Govindaraj): Drafted an initial plan for combined reasoning and state observability, under review by the group. The plan includes a gap analysis and landscape analysis, which will be opened as pull requests for review in August.


## Blockers and Risks
