## Summary
### Observability Working Group Updates
The meeting focused on updates from the Observability working group, including progress on taxonomy work and a report submitted to the Technical Committee. Pavan provided updates on taxonomy efforts, noting that many observability-relevant terms have been merged into universal terms, and Joe suggested reviewing terms marked as "debate" to identify additional relevant terms. Manu and Johanna presented on agent observability work, demonstrating a peer-to-peer observability stack for GossipSub and discussing measurement architectures, transport methods, and interoperability across different agent systems. Mirja from Ericsson provided context on related IETF work on accountability and measurement standards, while Steven shared insights on agent action capsules and CPV implementation. The discussion concluded with plans to review terms in the taxonomy sheet and continue work on an Observability SDK.

### Agent Observability and Metadata Discovery
Manu presented on agent observability and metadata discovery, discussing their work with Local AI and hyperscale AI projects on implementing OpenID and other ID formats for agent discovery. Steven provided feedback on VTO (Vehicle Telemetry Object) standards, emphasizing the separation of telemetry semantics from cryptography and highlighting its potential for creating portable artifacts. The team discussed extending their work in three different formats and shared learnings from their P2P ecosystem experiments, including network layer analysis and a roadmap for observability implementation.

### Observability Interoperability Proposal Discussion
Matt presented a proposal to build interoperability across existing libraries and components at the application layer, aiming to identify limitations and provide recommendations to the OTEL core spec while creating a working library. The team discussed updates from focus groups, with Govindaraj planning to work on feedback and schedule a presentation of the agent capsule, and Ty reporting progress on creating a modular Observability Map and a Context Intelligence Graph Agentic Runtime prototype. Pavan suggested creating a Discord poll to find alternative meeting times for the state and context group, given scheduling conflicts on Friday.

### Cross-Boundary Observability Collaboration Proposals
Pavan shared two proposals related to cross-boundary observability and ARD Observability, seeking feedback and potential collaboration with other work groups. Manu expressed interest in both proposals and mentioned connections with the identity working group at Agency, suggesting potential collaboration opportunities. Govindaraj highlighted overlaps between the presented topics and mentioned a new cross-workgroup initiative, offering to collaborate further on observability and networking efforts.

### LibP2P and Agentic Collaboration
The meeting focused on discussing collaboration opportunities in open source and the integration of LibP2P networking stack with the Agentic project. Manu and Johanna from the LibP2P team presented their work on peer-to-peer networking, including identity, discovery, routing, and communication protocols, as well as their observability tools that monitor metrics like throughput, latency, and bandwidth. The team discussed how their work could support the Agentic project's needs, particularly around semantics separation from crypto and the potential for integrating their observability stack with existing tools like CBOR and verifiable telemetry objects.

### Composable Trust Blueprint Development
Manu led a discussion on developing a composable trust blueprint combining Verifiable Telemetry Objects (VTO) and Agent Action Capsules (AAC) for interoperability in peer-to-peer systems. The team aligned on using CBOR42 for encoding telemetry and discussed the need to support floating-point numbers and refine registry entries. Key next steps include sharing the VTO specification, collaborating on an IETF draft for San Francisco, and experimenting with implementations in Python and Golang. The group agreed to open a pull request in the libp2p repository to integrate VTO with AAC, with a follow-up checkpoint meeting scheduled in two weeks.
