## Summary
### Observability Focus Group Updates
The team held a meeting with a light agenda focused on high-level updates from chairs meetings and updates from focus group leads. Matthew updated the community about two PRs (20 and 22) that he had submitted, and the group discussed reviewing these through comments rather than merging. Pavan mentioned a proposal from the multi-agent observability focus group that might be discussed if time permits, and noted that Manu from LibP2P would be presenting about work at the intersection of application layer and network stack observability.

### Taxonomy Work Progress Discussion
The team discussed progress on taxonomy work, with Matt and Matthew explaining that they are reviewing terms alphabetically from the Universal list, currently at the "agent" section. Matthew emphasized that observability and traceability are foundational working groups and suggested moving relevant terms to Universal to support other groups. Pavan shared that external contributors are currently not being accepted into repositories per guidance from Manik, the CTO, though this policy may change after discussions with the governing council. Matt noted that they are working on implementing an ad hoc meeting system to facilitate smaller group discussions before larger meetings.

### Focus Group Updates and Mergers
The team discussed updates from various focus groups, with Zayne reporting that two proposals from Pavan are under review regarding skills and agentic resource discovery pipeline. Matthew proposed merging the protocols group with primitives, emphasizing that this would help teams speak a unified language rather than reducing participation. The group also addressed scheduling challenges, with Sergey noting that recent holidays affected their working group meetings, and Matt confirming that while teams can use tools like Doodle for planning, they must use LF's scheduling system for actual meeting coordination. Pavan mentioned ongoing work on multi-agentic delegation semantics and trace context propagation, suggesting the focus group might be rolled into larger working groups due to lower attendance.

### Libp2p Agent Network Implementation
Manu presented on operating and measuring agent networks using libp2p at the peer-to-peer level, highlighting their implementation across 70-80 different blockchains and 11,500 GitHub projects. He discussed the technical architecture including networking, multi-formats, IPLD, and security components, and mentioned their tool Lumina designed specifically for agency. The discussion was cut short when Govindaraj raised a question about cryptographic solutions in the Spiffy framework potentially being redundant with existing work.

### Agent Interoperability and Identity Challenges
Govindaraj and Manu discussed identity and interoperability challenges in agent infrastructure, particularly when scaling across different hyperscalers like AWS and Google Cloud. They agreed to leverage existing OpenID specifications and standards like SSE for agent-to-agent communication to address security and trust issues. Manu committed to exploring OpenID specifications, NAT traversal methods, and SSE protocols, while also planning to share resources and materials on GitHub for other groups to reference.

### IETF Observability Standards Discussion
Manu explained that three IETF organizations are working on observability: Agent Proto, DMSC, and Dawn, with plans to present at IETF San Francisco. Matt asked about authority for observability data and reconciliation between differing traces in multi-agent environments, to which Manu responded that specific work on this is still pending within the agency group. The team discussed their scope of study, which currently focuses on application and peer-to-peer layers on AWS, with plans to expand to multiple hyperscalers including GCP and Akamai. Matt confirmed that the group plans to contribute to OpenTelemetry specifications and implementations, with Govindaraj supporting the need for standardized SDKs to ensure consistency across implementations.

### Security and Telemetry Standardization Discussion
Manu discussed recommendations for using Cbar 42 and mentioned plans to include email addresses and legal context protocols. Govindaraj suggested incorporating legal context protocols and proof of control mechanisms to enhance security and observability, recommending open-source tools like Praxen and Observer for forensics. Steven shared insights on identity-aware telemetry and proposed meeting with Manu to discuss potential collaboration on SCIT and IETF working groups. Manu emphasized the importance of focusing on standardization for exchange formats and telemetry representations rather than monitoring platforms.

### Agent Communication and Telemetry Updates
Manu presented on agent communication and telemetry work, covering 45 prepared slides including topics like CVAR 42, tooling implementations, and observability frameworks. The group agreed to schedule a follow-up session to dive deeper into specific areas, with Manu committing to share his email address and presentation materials including the IETF draft and tooling resources. Matt suggested connecting with the Identity and Trust Working Group regarding cryptographic verification of agent payloads and identity systems, and Pavan requested that Manu share the slides directly with him or Matt for distribution to the working group.
