# Overview

The meeting focused on updates and discussions for the observability and traceability working group, including proposed changes to focus groups and a presentation on the Agentic Resource Discovery (ARD) specification. The group decided to merge two overlapping focus groups (LLM Primitives and Protocol Observability) into a single combined group while maintaining their current meeting cadence, and also discussed potentially merging another focus group (Agent State, Context and Reasoning) into the combined group. Junjie presented the ARD specification, which was announced jointly with 10 other companies and aims to help agents discover and verify AI resources through a decentralized system using catalogs and dynamic registries, with built-in trust mechanisms and support for standards like ANS and SPIFFE. The meeting also covered updates on taxonomy work, tool contributions including Matthew's new skills for landscape analysis, and plans for future discussions about Agentic trace models.

## Agentic Resource Discovery Specification

Junjie presented the Agentic Resource Discovery (ARD) specification, which aims to help agents discover and verify AI resources through two complementary components:

- Catalog (static indexing)
- Registry (dynamic registry with REST API)

The specification supports decentralized trust models using SPIFFE and ANS standards, with natural language search capabilities and verification mechanisms through trust manifests. The discussion revealed potential overlaps with existing working groups, particularly around ANS standards and agent registry efforts, with participants agreeing to follow up asynchronously about identification standards and traceability models through the Discord channel.
