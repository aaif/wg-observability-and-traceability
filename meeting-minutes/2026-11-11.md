## Summary

### AAIF Technical Program Updates
The meeting began with introductions, including Dawn Wageshear introducing herself as the new technical program manager for the AAIF, who will work to reduce friction and increase incentives for project development. The group discussed the light attendance due to KubeCon and Veterans Day, but agreed to proceed with updates on deliverables and focus groups. The meeting was about to begin with updates on taxonomy when the transcript ended.

### Taxonomy and Automation Updates
Joe announced he finally received permissions to manipulate the board and create automation for ticketing and assignment. The team discussed decisions from a recent taxonomy meeting, including the agreement to use one PR per term rather than combining multiple terms. Matt suggested structuring the approach to presenting terms to the taxonomy group with well-thought-through definitions and scope notes rather than submitting them in multiple steps. The conversation also touched on Matthew's website integration PR, which Pavan confirmed was already approved by Juliana.

### Agentic AI Foundation Terminology Discussion
The team discussed merging a pull request that failed checks due to rendering issues, with Matt confirming he would follow up and fix the problems in a subsequent PR. The group addressed terminology gaps in defining concepts like "agent" and "turn" within the Agentic AI Foundation, with Matt explaining that while OTel has some definitions, they lack semantic consistency, making collaboration with workflow teams important. Victor noted that for AI security terminology, there is greater agreement between OTel, OCISF and COSI, with COSI likely defining the matrix terminology.

### ATIF v2 Standardization Collaboration
Sujay discussed working with Harbor on ATIF v2 for agent trajectories, proposing to standardize the definition and data model across the industry. Matt expressed alignment with the goal of standardizing semantics in OpenTelemetry (OTEL) to enable seamless translation into trajectory formats. They agreed to schedule a 15-20 minute discussion in a future meeting to introduce the topic and explore collaboration between OTEL and ATIF v2.

### Trajectory Data Handling Approaches
The group discussed approaches for handling trajectory data, with opinions divided between sending data as log events versus introducing a new signal. Sujay mentioned that ATIF v2 currently exists as a public repository without a specific home foundation, and suggested joining discussions in the ATIF v2 space. Matt explained that while traces can model various concepts, longer-running autonomous workflows may require different formats, and noted that OTel traces remain the de facto standard for data publication in GenAI observability. The participants agreed to continue discussions through pull requests and potentially establish a more persistent working group focused on trajectories.

### AI Standardization and Context Graph
Pavan discussed interest in creating a trajectory map for AI agents and standardizing workflows, while Ty presented his "patchwork AI" project to coordinate and understand overlaps between AI standardization bodies by creating a context graph. Ty also shared details about a context graph benchmark he developed, which includes testing for tokens to correct answers, swarm testing with 12 agents, and a 24-hour soak test with a million events, aiming to establish a standardization tool for context graph libraries.

### Code Review and Deliverables Planning
Ty shared a PDF document containing working deterministic code and discussed using local downloads instead of GitHub's GraphQL endpoints for AI processing. The team discussed continuing with their meeting agenda, with Zayne providing an update that the group is now identifying gaps for deliverables and asking members to review the proposal document asynchronously to determine if they are close to a V1 version.

### Working Group Deliverables Planning
The team discussed plans for sharing working group deliverables with broader audiences, with Matt and Pavan agreeing to explore formats beyond GitHub for more easily consumable outputs. Govindaraj reported progress on aligning context graph and evidence layer requirements, which will be discussed further with the team tomorrow.
