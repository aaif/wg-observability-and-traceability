The group also discussed focus group meetings for Agent State Context and Reasoning, and considered creating a new focus group on observability across different layers of the technology stack, particularly for non-application layers from L6 downwards.

### Observability Strategy Discussion
The team discussed focused efforts on observability, particularly at different layers including network and system levels. Manu shared that they are collaborating with Steven's team on ActionState and attended a meeting where a PR was discussed that showed strong alignment with peer-to-peer networks. The group explored the need for a potential second focus group around security and compliance for observability, and discussed how to enable tracing for MCP apps, extensions, and Agent Gateway. Pavan and Joe emphasized the importance of including system-level traceability and observability for a complete end-to-end story, while Trace suggested focusing on well-defined primitives and protocols at lower layers.

### Observability Focus Group Leadership
The team discussed the observability focus group and agreed to have Trace lead the provenance initiative across the entire stack. They determined that Sal's work on lower-layer observability at the syscall level would be a good fit for the existing focus group, with additional participation in the Protocol Observability focus group and Security and Privacy Cross working group also recommended. The group emphasized the importance of cross-working group collaboration to ensure consistency in foundation efforts, and agreed to proceed with Trace leading the provenance initiative in the protocol group.

### Agent Action Capsule Project Presentation
Steven presented the Agent Action Capsule project, explaining how it serves as a middle layer between regulations and agent actions by recording what was dispatched, what happened, and whether it was allowed to happen. He demonstrated the implementation using MeshLLM, showing how capsules can be verified and how the system prevents tampering through cryptographic proofs. The discussion included questions about reconstructing complete execution from multiple capsules, with Steven confirming this is easily achievable through the ledger system, and explored potential collaborations with various organizations including Cisco's Internet of Agents initiative and the government of Taipei.
