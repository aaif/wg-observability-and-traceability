The team discussed the strategy for supporting languages in the AAC and SDKs project. Pavan discussed ongoing agency projects focused on developing comprehensive observability for single-agent and multi-agent systems, particularly for Open Claw-related systems like Nemo Claw and Pico Claw. Steven mentioned their team's work on Open Claw, Hermes, Nemo, and Zero Claw projects, expressing willingness to compare notes and review repositories. The group agreed to review a hotel proposal and an initial white paper draft that Manu has been working on, with plans to share it for feedback from the workgroup. Pavan also noted that multiple workgroups showed interest in combining efforts on attestation, with Steven having submitted a proposal for an explicit workgroup with liaisons from different groups including security and privacy.

### AI Attestation Working Group Proposal

Steven reported on a meeting with the Advanced AI Society proof of control team regarding a proposed working group on attestation. The group discussed starting a 90-day trial to evaluate whether this should become a work stream, working group, or cross-working group, with the possibility of beginning as an attestation working group. Govindaraj emphasized the industry need for better attestation and verification in this area, while Pavan noted strong interest among current workgroup members in the attestation and evidence space.

### Working Group Reorganization Discussion

The team discussed reorganizing working groups to address effectiveness issues and improve collaboration between projects and working groups. Matt presented a proposal to define the concept of a "turn" in agent interactions to establish consistent semantic conventions for tracing across different platforms. The group agreed to develop a proof of concept and matrix mapping current SDK implementations before submitting to the OpenTelemetry GenAI SIG. Next steps include reviewing the proposal comments, creating a PR for the semantic convention repository, and potentially discussing cross-domain agent interactions and security considerations in future meetings.
