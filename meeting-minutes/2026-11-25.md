## Key Topics

### Cross-Working Group Synergies Discussion

The group discussed potential synergies between different working groups, particularly around APAC meetings, with Matthew suggesting a cross-working group meeting to increase attendance and engagement. Sujay announced plans to introduce ATIF V2 to the group, and Joe shared updates about hiring a new team member to focus on Observability work while transitioning himself to the taxonomy workstream. The team also discussed concerns about loop transformers potentially affecting Observability, with Matt and Ty explaining that current Observability focuses on outcomes rather than internal computation, and Ty noting that good traceability and security can actually reduce token usage by up to 70%. The conversation ended with discussions about taxonomy definitions and a white paper deliverable, with Matt agreeing to create a draft PR for review and the group planning to develop worked examples for different sections of the Observability paper.

### White Paper and ATIF V2 Discussion

The team discussed approaches for contributing to a white paper, with Pavan suggesting comments on the PR would be helpful and Manu confirming interest in a RFC-style document for submission to IETF working groups 128 and 129. Matt proposed discussing deliverable mechanisms in their monthly report to gather TC feedback. The meeting then transitioned to Sujay introducing work on defining ATIF V2, with Pereira explaining Google's approach to evolving their observability needs and partnership with Harbor, focusing on streaming support, lineage improvements, and semantic definitions for trajectory formats.

### OpenTelemetry Trajectory Proposal Discussion

Pereira discussed a proposal and sought feedback on making it work across the industry, particularly focusing on open-sourcing tool sets and converting across different agent harnesses. Liudmila explained her work on OpenTelemetry, GenAI, and semantic conventions, proposing that trajectories should be a focused view built from OpenTelemetry telemetry, with the goal of defining a mapping from telemetry to trajectories. She suggested that OpenTelemetry could produce trajectory events with a specific event format to address the unique needs of harnesses and agents.

### GenAI Event Stream Format Discussion

Matt discussed the challenges of introducing a new event stream format for GenAI semantic conventions and proposed leveraging OpenTelemetry (OTel) traces as the interchange format instead. Pereira agreed that the new trajectory format should complement existing OTel traces rather than replace them, emphasizing that it should not be an either/or situation. Pavan asked about ATIF V2's support for nonlinear execution and multi-agentic systems, to which Pereira confirmed that it should support these cases, though some specific implementations may still need to be determined. Matt suggested formalizing certain event definitions, like turns and sessions, in the canonical trace format to enable lossless conversion to trajectories.

### ATIF V2 Implementation Discussion

The team discussed implementing ATIF V2 for agent action tracking in OpenTelemetry logs, with Matt emphasizing the need for community alignment on a single standard rather than creating multiple formats. Pereira confirmed that Google is moving forward with ATIF V2 after discussions with the Harbor team, though some implementation details around canonical bytes and stable digests remain unclear and need further exploration. Sujay suggested continuing the conversation through the OTel GenAI SIG forum and a new ATIF V2 repository, while Alan noted the need to first stabilize the current implementation before expanding collaboration on additional features.

### AI Trajectory and OTel Integration

The group discussed how to integrate AI trajectory formats with OpenTelemetry (OTel) standards, with Matt suggesting using OTel as the wire format and trajectories as the storage format. Liudmila confirmed that trajectories can live within OTel, and the team agreed to explore making the Harbor repo a collaboration space while determining a permanent home for the initiative. Matt mentioned he would share a document surveying agent SDKs' OTel conformance issues, and the group decided to discuss next steps at their next meeting, including where to establish the initiative and define processes.
